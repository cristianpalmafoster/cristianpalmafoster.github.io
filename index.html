About

Cristian J. Palma Foster

Hey! I'm a third-year PhD student in Operations Research at Cornell University, and very fortunate to be advised by Éva Tardos. My research interests include algorithmic game theory, learning in games, online learning and bandits, approximation algorithms, and combinatorial optimization.

Previously, I studied Mathematical Engineering at Universidad de Chile and École CentraleSupélec, and later earned an MSc in Applied Mathematics at Universidad de Chile, advised by José Soto.
